Basics of Variables Algorithm

Step 1:
Start the program, including necessary header files and the 'main' function.
Step 2:
Declare the variables -
int a, b, sum;
float celsius, fahrenheit, marks, percentage;
Step 3:
Take inputs for variables 'a' and 'b'.
Step 4:
Calculate sum of 'a' and 'b' and store it in 'sum'.
Step 5:
Display 'sum'.
Step 6:
Take input for variable 'celsius'.
Step 7:
Calculate - (celsius X 9 5 ) + 32 and store it in 'fahrenheit'.
Step 8:
Display 'fahrenheit'.
Step 9:
Take input for variable 'marks'.
Step 10:
Calculate - marks 500 X 100 and store it in 'percentage'.
Step 11:
Display 'percentage'.
Step 12:
End the program.
